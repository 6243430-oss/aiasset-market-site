How much money do I need to start operating AI assets? +

Depends on your strategy. Some operators start by taking over abandoned projects for free (the builder is happy someone will maintain it). Others buy assets in the $500–$5,000 range. The course covers both paths — and how to evaluate risk at each level.

AI assets often depend heavily on their creator — the prompts, the workflow, the specific model choice. If those aren't documented and transferable, you're not buying an asset, you're buying a demo. This course teaches you exactly how to tell the difference before you sign anything.
