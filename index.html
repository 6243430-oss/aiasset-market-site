Pirate Grade

How we evaluate every submission

5 dimensions. Honest assessment. We review claims and map scenarios — we do not guarantee outcomes.

📡 Demand Signal
Are there signs someone wants this? Existing audience, buyer requests, search demand, proven use case.
📦 Asset Readiness
Is it already an asset or still an idea? Launched, documented, transferable — vs. rough prototype.
🧑‍✈️ Operator Fit
Can someone else run it without the founder? Clear SOP, low specialist dependency, documented workflow.
🚀 Upside Paths
What scenarios could make it more valuable? Market opportunity, monetization levers, growth options.
🛡️ Risk & Proof
What is claimed vs. reviewed. Platform risk, legal exposure, ownership verification, transfer feasibility.
A — Strong B — Interesting C — Early D — Not Ready ✕ Reject

Grade is not fixed. Sellers can improve their Pirate Grade by adding transfer notes, upside paths, operator SOPs or verified evidence. It reflects what you document and share.

Pirate Grade is the editorial review label by Pirate AI Port — the community behind AIAsset.Market.
