§ 08 · СРОКИ ХРАНЕНИЯ

Как долго мы храним данные

  • Данные Free Audit и Buyer Intent: до отзыва согласия или 3 (три) года с момента последнего взаимодействия — что наступит раньше
  • Email подписчиков Weekly Drop: до отзыва согласия (клик по ссылке «Unsubscribe» в письме)
  • Данные, отправленные в AI Asset Score: не сохраняются — обрабатываются в моменте и удаляются после возврата результата
  • IP-адрес для rate-limiting: 24 часа
  • Логи серверные (CF Insights): согласно политике Cloudflare — до 30 дней

По истечении срока хранения либо при отзыве согласия персональные данные подлежат уничтожению в течение 30 календарных дней.

§ EN · INFORMATIONAL

Privacy Policy — English summary

The Russian version above is the legally binding text under Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ. This English summary is provided for informational purposes.

Who we are

AIAsset.Market is a personal brokerage for AI-native assets, operated by Maxim Napoykin (Russia, Moscow). Contact: hi@aiasset.market · Telegram @Pirate_AI.

What we collect

Only what you voluntarily submit via forms (Free Audit, AI Asset Score, Buyer Intent, Weekly Drop): name, contact (email / Telegram handle), asset details (URL, category, revenue metrics). Automatic technical data: IP (for rate-limiting), user-agent, referrer, UTM. One localStorage key for cookie consent. No tracking cookies, no advertising pixels. Analytics via Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookieless, no cross-site tracking). The homepage loads a YouTube thumbnail from Google's CDN on page load (i.ytimg.com); the full YouTube embed activates only when you click play.

What we use it for

To review your submission, grade your asset, contact you about it, deliver Weekly Drop if you subscribed, publish aggregated anonymized stats. No profiling, no automated decisions, no behavioral advertising.

Cross-border transfers

Your data is transferred to processors in the United States: Cloudflare (CDN + hosting + analytics), Google (Fonts + Sheets backup during migration to RU cloud + YouTube thumbnail CDN on page load + YouTube embed on play), OpenRouter (AI Asset Score — receives your URL, description, metrics, and up to 4KB of page content; not stored after response). See aiasset.market/consent/ for the separate consent form covering cross-border transfers.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time. Write to hi@aiasset.market. We respond within 10 business days. If you're an EU resident, you have equivalent rights under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection).

Retention

Form submissions: 3 years or until you revoke consent. Weekly Drop email: until you unsubscribe (one-click). AI Asset Score data: not stored at all. IP for rate-limit: 24 hours. Server logs: max 30 days.

How to revoke consent

Email hi@aiasset.market with subject «Revoke consent». We stop processing on receipt and delete your data within 30 days.
