What is a AI Asset Score?

A AI Asset Score is a structured 7-dimension audit of an AI project. It scores 0–100 and answers one question: could someone other than the founder operate this, grow it, or buy it?

Most AI projects fail on transferability — the founder is essential to the system working. A AI Asset Score makes that visible immediately, and tells you what it would take to fix it.

Grade A (75+) projects get introduced to active buyers and operators. Grade B (60–74) projects go on the watchlist and improve with specific feedback.

What typically passes — and what fails

✓ Passes

  • Has a working runbook someone else used
  • Revenue from multiple unrelated customers
  • 90+ days of consistent usage or payment history
  • One clear customer type and why they pay
  • Setup completes in under 2 hours from docs alone
  • Prompts and workflows are exported/owned

✗ Fails

  • Founder is the only one who knows how it works
  • Revenue from one client who knows you personally
  • Best-month screenshot instead of consistent data
  • "Anyone who needs AI" as the customer definition
  • Setup requires a call with you to explain it
  • Core prompts stored in a personal ChatGPT account

Frequently asked questions

Is the AI Asset Score really free?

Yes. Free for every submission. The grade is our cost of building trust with builders and founders. We earn when we facilitate a deal — not from charging for the audit.

How long does a grade take?

Typically 24–48 hours. During an active Drop window (when weekly submissions are open), we prioritize grades so you can be included if you score 75+.

Do I have to be planning to sell to get graded?

No. A grade is useful even if you're not selling yet. Most founders use it to understand what's missing before they approach buyers — or to decide whether to keep building or move on.

What if I score low?

Low grades include specific notes on what's dragging the score down and what it would take to fix. A 45 today with clear feedback is more actionable than no information at all.

Can I submit something I found — not something I built?

Yes. Scout submissions are welcome. If you find a project that looks like an asset and want to know if it qualifies, submit it. If it grades A and leads to a deal, scouts are credited.

What does the grade tell me that I don't already know?

It tells you what a buyer or operator would see — not what you, as the founder, know it could be. Those two views are usually very different, and the gap between them is what the grade measures.
