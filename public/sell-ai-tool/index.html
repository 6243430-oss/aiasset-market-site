Why most AI tools undersell

The gap between what founders ask and what buyers pay comes down to one thing: documentation.

What sells at 15–20× MRR

  • ✓ Works without the founder explaining anything
  • ✓ One sentence that describes what it does, for who
  • ✓ Revenue documented with Stripe or bank statements
  • ✓ A new user can onboard in under 15 minutes
  • ✓ Prompts, IP and accounts all transferable
  • ✓ External demand signal (reviews, inbound, community)

What sells at 2–5× MRR (if at all)

  • ✗ Needs a 2-hour call to explain the product
  • ✗ "It's basically like X but with AI" positioning
  • ✗ Revenue is verbal — no paper trail
  • ✗ Setup requires founder's personal accounts
  • ✗ Codebase is undocumented, can't be handed over
  • ✗ Only social proof is the founder's own posts

What types of AI tools can be sold

Any AI-powered product with at least one paying customer or 100 active users.

AI SaaS

Subscription products with an AI core — not just an AI widget. If removing the AI makes the product stop working, it qualifies.

Automation / Agent

n8n, Make, Zapier workflows. LangChain pipelines. Custom Python automations with external clients. Any AI system that runs without the user present.

AI Newsletter / Channel

AI-generated or AI-curated publications with real subscribers. The content system must be documented and reproducible by a new operator.

GPT Wrapper / Prompt Pack

A tuned interface around an LLM for a specific use case. Valuable when the prompt engineering is non-obvious and the user base is established.

No-Code AI App

Bubble, Webflow, Glide, or other no-code platforms with an AI integration. Highly transferable — no developer needed for handoff.

AI Data / Persona

Trained models, fine-tuned LLMs, curated datasets, or AI persona systems with documented performance and a clear deployment path.

Frequently asked

My tool is pre-revenue. Can I still sell it?

Yes. Pre-revenue tools are graded and listed. They score lower on criterion 3 (Revenue Proof) but can still reach B grade and attract operators who want revenue share rather than a flat acquisition. A tool with 200 active free users and a clear monetization path is sellable.

What's the minimum revenue to get a good grade?

No hard minimum. Even $300/month with 3 months of Stripe history beats "we have 10 verbal commitments." The grade rewards documented proof — not scale. We've graded a $400 MRR tool that scored higher than a $3k MRR tool because the documentation was better.

Do you charge for the grade?

No. The AI Asset Score is free. We charge only if we broker a transaction — 5% for deals under $50k, 10% for deals above. If no deal closes, you pay nothing.

How long does it take to sell?

Timing depends on evidence quality, buyer interest, due diligence, and transfer complexity. An AI Asset Score does not guarantee an introduction or sale.

Can I sell part of it? Or just find an operator?

Yes to both. Full acquisition, partial stake, and operator matching are all options. Operator deals are especially common for tools with 100–1000 users that haven't monetized yet — the operator runs and grows the tool in exchange for a revenue share.

My code is a mess. Does that matter?

Less than you think. Buyers care about what the tool does, not how the code is written — unless the buyer needs to extend it. What matters: it runs reliably without you, and someone can understand what it does from the outside. Clean docs beat clean code every time in the $5k–$30k range.
